What runs today

Capabilities that are built and working — with their current limits stated.

Plan rendering & sheet navigation

Multi-page PDFs and images render in the browser with a page picker. Scale is held per sheet, so changing pages resets calibration.

DWG/DXF upload is accepted and stored, but tracing on them is not supported yet.

Calibrated takeoffs

Set scale off a known dimension, then trace a closed area or an open run. Square footage and linear footage are recomputed server-side — the browser's number is never trusted.

Scale entry is manual calibration; automatic scale detection is not built. No count tool yet.

AI plan summary

At upload, Claude reads the batch and drafts scope, materials, missing information, and constructability concerns across the sheets.

Advisory only: no scale verification, no markup proof. Not a deliverable quantity.

Subcontract drafting

A guided question-and-answer flow fills the AMS master subcontract and returns a finished .docx, ready to send.

Scope is entered in the chat — takeoff values are not wired into it yet.

Markup proof & review log

Every saved takeoff stores a snapshot of the calibration line and traced boundary over the plan, plus a confidence rating with its reasons. Approve or correct it, and the change is logged.

Confidence is capped at medium — no automated cross-check exists yet.
