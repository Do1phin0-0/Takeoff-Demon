# Easily Missed Master Checklist

Run this against every priced estimate. List anything not yet covered as "Possibly Missing" bullets in the Risks section of the output.

Run the section that matches the detected project type. For mixed projects, run all relevant sections.

---

# RESIDENTIAL ITEMS

---

## Soft Costs
- Building permit
- Plan review fees
- Impact fees / development fees
- Engineering (structural, MEP, energy)
- Surveying / staking
- Soil testing / geotech
- Termite pretreat
- Builder's risk insurance
- General liability rider for project
- Bond (if required)

## Site / General Conditions
- Mobilization / demobilization
- Temp power
- Temp water
- Port-a-john (per month, qty)
- Dumpster (per phase: demo, framing, drywall, finish)
- Silt fence / SWPPP / erosion control
- Tree protection fencing
- Construction signage
- Final clean (interior + exterior)
- Construction cleaning during build (rough, intermediate)
- Punch list reserve
- Project management / supervision
- Contingency (5–10% recommended)

## Hardware / Connectors
- Simpson hangers (joist, beam, post)
- Hold-downs (HDU, MSTC)
- Straps (LSTA, MSTA, CMSTC)
- Anchor bolts (qty + size)
- Fasteners by the lb (8d, 10d, 16d, ring shank)
- Sealants (silicone, polyurethane, butyl)
- Adhesives (PL, construction adhesive)
- Weatherstripping
- Caulking and backer rod

## Backing / Blocking
- Grab bars (bath walls, shower)
- Wall-mount TVs (bedrooms, living, primary)
- Cabinets (upper and base)
- Closet shelving
- Handrails (stairs, halls)
- Towel bars and accessories
- Curtain / drapery rods
- Attic ladder framing

## Code-Driven Adds
- AFCI breakers per code
- GFCI receptacles (kitchen, bath, exterior, garage, laundry, basement)
- Smoke/CO interconnect, hardwired with battery backup
- Fireblocking (concealed spaces, top/bottom of walls)
- Draftstopping (large concealed spaces)
- Egress windows (basements, bedrooms below grade)
- Tempered glass at hazardous locations (near doors, tubs, stairs)
- Ice & water shield by climate zone (eaves, valleys, penetrations)
- Fresh-air ventilation per IRC (whole-house ventilation)
- Whole-house ventilation control

## MEP Wedge Items
- Range hood duct to exterior (with damper)
- Bath fan duct to exterior with backdraft damper
- Dryer vent (with damper, exterior cap)
- Condensate float switch on HVAC
- Water hammer arrestors (washing machine, dishwasher)
- Frost-proof hose bibs (qty front/back/sides)
- Exterior GFCIs (front, back, sides)
- Dedicated appliance circuits (microwave, fridge, DW, disposal)
- Doorbell circuit + transformer
- Garage door opener outlets (per door)
- Attic light + outlet near furnace
- Photocell or timer for exterior lights
- Whole-house surge protector (some codes)

## Finish Wedge Items
- Shower waterproofing membrane (Schluter, Kerdi, RedGard)
- Tile substrate (cement board, uncoupling membrane)
- Anti-fracture membrane on cracked slabs
- Floor transitions and threshold strips
- Stair nosing
- Paint touch-up after flooring install
- Closet shelving and rods
- Door stops at every door
- Address numbers
- Mailbox + post + concrete
- Driveway approach / culvert (per local code)
- Final landscaping / sod / final grade
- Sidewalk and patio

# COMMERCIAL ITEMS

---

## Commercial Soft Costs
- Plan review fees (city / county / state)
- Site plan approval / variance fees
- Impact fees / capital improvement fees
- Civil engineering (paving, drainage, utility design)
- Structural engineering (delegated design — steel connections, CFMF)
- MEP engineering and PE stamps
- Energy modeling (COMcheck or full energy model)
- Geotech investigation report
- Environmental Phase I / Phase II
- TDLR plan review (Texas Department of Licensing — RAS/PRAC)
- Health department permits (food service)
- Fire marshal plan review and inspection
- Builder's risk insurance (commercial — higher rate than residential)
- Performance bond + payment bond (often required on commercial)
- Bid bond
- Surveying — initial topo, layout, as-built, final survey

## Commercial General Conditions
- Project superintendent (full-time on-site)
- Project manager allocation
- Mobilization / job trailer
- Job trailer rental (months)
- Temporary office utilities (power, internet)
- Site fencing (construction, separate from permanent)
- Construction signage per OSHA + city
- Temp toilets (qty per worker count, OSHA requires 1 per 20)
- Dumpsters (multiple per phase)
- Job clean (weekly)
- Final cleaning (commercial — windows inside/out, polish floors, etc.)
- Builder's risk insurance allocation
- General liability allocation
- Equipment allowance (forklift rental, scissor lift, scaffolding)
- Crane rental for steel erection (if needed)
- Hoisting allowance for cladding / heavy equipment
- Temporary heat/cooling for finish work
- First aid station / safety officer per OSHA threshold
- Drug testing (if required)
- Background checks (some sites)
- Punch list reserve (1-2% typical)
- Warranty reserve (1-2% typical)
- Project contingency 5-10%
- Schedule contingency (weather days, especially earthwork phases)

## Steel & Inspections
- AESS Category upcharge (if architectural exposed) — 25% to 100% premium
- Special inspections (steel) — third-party agency, paid by GC
- Weld testing — UT (ultrasonic) for full-penetration welds, MT/PT for partial-penetration
- High-strength bolt verification (slip-critical connections)
- Anchor bolt installation inspection
- Concrete inspections (slumps, cylinders, in-place testing)
- Soil compaction testing (each lift)
- Lime/cement stabilization testing (gradation, moisture, density)
- Asphalt density testing (cores)
- Pavement thickness verification

## Fire & Life Safety
- Fire sprinkler design / submittals / install (separate sub typically)
- Fire alarm design / submittals / install
- Fire pump and controller (if required by code)
- Fire department connection (FDC) and signage
- Sprinkler tap fees
- Fire-rated assemblies — verify gypsum type X, fire caulking at penetrations
- Smoke barriers (often confused with fire barriers)
- Fire dampers at duct penetrations of fire-rated walls
- Smoke dampers at smoke barriers
- Through-penetration firestop systems (UL-listed assemblies)
- Top-of-wall (head-of-wall) joints in fire/smoke barriers
- Exit signage and emergency lighting per egress plan
- Manual pull stations at every exit
- Fire extinguishers (type and qty per fire code)
- Fire watch during hot work
- Annual fire inspection deficiencies (existing buildings)

## ADA / Accessibility
- ADA-compliant accessible route from public way to entry
- ADA-accessible parking count (per total spaces)
- Van-accessible parking (8-ft stall + 8-ft access aisle for van)
- ADA-accessible signage (van-accessible, ISA symbol)
- Accessible route — slope, cross slope, surface, width
- Truncated dome detectable warning at curb ramps and platform edges
- ADA toilet rooms — clearances, grab bars, mirror height, partition height, hardware reach
- Accessible drinking fountain (hi/lo)
- Accessible threshold heights (1/2" max)
- Accessible door hardware (lever, no twisting)
- Accessible counters / service counters
- Visual + audible fire alarms (ADA strobes)

## Commercial MEP Wedge Items
- Transformer pad and service entrance conduits (utility coordination)
- CT cabinet and current transformers
- SPD (surge protective device) at service
- Arc-flash labeling and study
- Emergency power off (EPO) where required
- Generator pad, fuel tank, and exhaust
- ATS (automatic transfer switch)
- 277V vs 120V circuiting (commercial)
- Site lighting circuits and photocell/timer
- Exterior receptacles GFCI weatherproof while-in-use covers
- RTU disconnects mounted on each unit (NEC required)
- Outside air per ASHRAE 62.1 for occupancy
- Economizer per climate zone
- Fresh air intake at proper distance from exhaust
- Trap primers at all floor drains
- Backflow preventer at water service (RPZ assembly + annual testing)
- Grease interceptor sizing per local plumbing code (food service)
- Indirect waste from kitchen equipment
- Hose bibs at exterior (qty + frost-proof)
- Mop sink in janitor closet
- Roof drain leaders inside building (vs. external scuppers)
- Recirculation pump for hot water (commercial code)
- Expansion tank on water heater (closed system)

## Commercial Cladding Wedge
- Mock-up panel approval (often required)
- Color/finish sample approval and lead time
- Panel layout/coursing approval
- Transition flashings between dissimilar materials
- Panel system engineering (delegated)
- Drip edge above all openings
- Sealant/backer rod at all panel joints (separate from perimeter)
- Soffit panels at overhangs
- Water table cap at base of cladding
- Returns at openings (jamb depth)

## Commercial Roofing Wedge
- RTU curbs and curb extensions (qty per RTU)
- Pipe penetration flashings (every plumbing vent, all gas piping)
- Duct penetrations and curb-mounted equipment
- Expansion joints across structural movement (where shown on S-drawings)
- Walkway pads at servicing routes (HVAC, satellite, antennas)
- Snow guards (climate-dependent)
- Fall protection anchors (OSHA 1910 / 1926)
- Lightning protection
- NDL (no-dollar-limit) warranty inspection visits
- Manufacturer pre-roofing meeting requirement
- Infrared scan after install (some warranties)
- Bird/critter exclusion at vents
- Drain strainers and overflow scuppers

## Commercial Finishes Wedge
- ACT cuts/borders at perimeter walls
- Fire-rated ACT systems where above corridors or in rated assemblies
- Hold-downs / seismic clips on ACT (climate-dependent)
- Transitions between flooring types (Schluter or transition strips)
- Slab moisture testing before flooring install (calcium chloride or RH)
- Slab flatness testing (FF/FL numbers per spec)
- Slab leveling compound where out of tolerance
- Hollow metal frame anchoring per substrate (welded to steel, bolted to CFMF, masonry anchors)
- Door silencers on every metal frame
- Kickplates on high-traffic doors
- ADA threshold heights at exterior doors
- Weatherstripping and door bottoms

# CIVIL / SITEWORK ITEMS

---

## Civil Soft Costs / Permits
- Civil engineering plan production
- Land survey (boundary, topo, as-built)
- Geotech report (soil borings, lab testing)
- Environmental Phase I / II
- TxDOT permit fees (driveway, encroachment) — TR permits
- City stormwater permit (NOI)
- City right-of-way permit (excavation in ROW)
- Tree preservation / mitigation fees
- Wetland delineation if applicable
- Floodplain permit if applicable
- Utility tap fees (water, sewer, gas, electric)
- Construction water meter setup

## Civil General Conditions / Site Setup
- Mobilization (excavator, dozer, scrapers, water truck)
- Demobilization
- Job trailer + utilities
- Temporary fencing around construction zone
- Construction signage
- Temp port-a-johns
- Temp construction power (not always needed for civil-only)
- Temp construction water and metering
- Stabilized construction entrance maintenance
- Mud tracking cleanup on adjacent roads (city often fines)
- Dust control (water truck, polymer)
- Traffic control during work in or near ROW
- Daily site security if equipment stored on-site

## Earthwork Wedge Items
- Topsoil strip and stockpile vs export
- Cut/fill imbalance — import or export quantities
- Unsuitable soil overdig and re-fill (allowance)
- Rock excavation (allowance)
- Dewatering (pumps, piping, discharge permit)
- Compaction testing per lift (paid by GC)
- Final grade tolerance per civil plans (typically ±0.1 ft)
- Topsoil respread on disturbed areas
- Stockpile management and overhead haul

## Storm / Utility Wedge Items
- Trench shoring at depths >5 ft (OSHA Subpart P)
- Bedding stone qty (typically #57 or #67, per detail)
- Pipe class verification (Class III RCP standard, IV/V at deep depths)
- Connection to existing manhole (core drill + boot, often forgotten)
- Drop manhole at large elevation differences (>2 ft)
- Manhole frame and cover finished elevation matching final pavement
- Inlet grate traffic rating (HS-20 vehicular vs. pedestrian)
- Sumps in manholes if specified
- Riprap at outfall with filter fabric (cy)
- Anti-seep collars on outfall pipes through embankment dams
- Headwall reinforcement and footing
- Video inspection (CCTV) of storm sewer post-install (often spec'd)
- Manhole vacuum testing or hydrostatic testing
- Hydrant flushing required after install
- Tracer wire on non-metallic pipe (gas, water)
- As-built survey of installed utilities (geo-locate manholes/inlets)

## Pavement Wedge Items
- Lime stabilization vs cement stabilization (depth and %)
- Curing time before placing pavement on stabilized subgrade (3-7 days)
- Aggregate base course gradation per TxDOT or local spec
- Concrete strength verification (cylinders at 7/28 days)
- Dowel basket assemblies at construction joints (every 60-100 ft)
- Isolation joints at every fixed structure (curb, manhole, light pole)
- Saw cut control joints — timing critical (4-12 hrs after pour)
- Backer rod and joint sealant at expansion joints (often forgotten)
- Curing compound application
- Surface texture finish (broom direction perpendicular to traffic)
- Pavement striping cure time before traffic (24-72 hrs)
- Concrete washout disposal
- Heat/cold weather concrete additives
- Sealing of asphalt pavement (slurry seal, fog seal — typically 1 yr post)

## SWPPP Wedge Items
- SWPPP plan preparation (engineer or QSP)
- NOI (Notice of Intent) filing — TPDES general permit in Texas
- Periodic SWPPP inspections (weekly + after 1/2" rain events)
- Inspection logs and corrective actions documentation
- Repairs to silt fence after rain events (recurring cost)
- Additional inlet protection as inlets are added during construction
- Off-site mud cleanup on adjacent streets
- Final stabilization (perennial vegetation 70% cover before NOT filing)
- NOT (Notice of Termination) filing
- Permanent BMPs (detention, water quality features) — often separate scope

## Fencing / Site Amenity Wedge Items
- Heavier corner/end posts vs. line posts
- Gate post footings sized larger
- Gate operator electrical conduit and trenching
- Gate safety devices (photo eyes, edges, loop detectors) — UL 325 compliance
- Fall protection during install at heights
- Post depth in heavy clay or rocky soil (deeper or rock auger)
- Painting / vinyl-coating upgrade if specified
- Privacy slats or windscreen if specified

## Striping / Marking Wedge Items
- ADA parking count (1 per 25 standard for first 100 spaces)
- Van-accessible parking (1 per 6 ADA spaces, 11x18 stall + 8 ft access aisle)
- Fire lane painting and signage per local fire marshal
- Stop bars at all egress points
- Directional arrows and traffic flow markings
- Striping cure time before traffic (especially thermoplastic)
- Existing pavement removal/grinding for re-stripe
