# Easily Missed Master Checklist

Run this against every priced estimate. List anything not yet covered as "Possibly Missing" bullets in the Risks section of the output.

---

## Soft Costs
- Building permit
- Plan review fees
- Impact fees / development fees
- Engineering (structural, MEP, energy)
- Surveying / staking
- Soil testing / geotech
- Termite pretreat
- Builder's risk insurance
- General liability rider for project
- Bond (if required)

## Site / General Conditions
- Mobilization / demobilization
- Temp power
- Temp water
- Port-a-john (per month, qty)
- Dumpster (per phase: demo, framing, drywall, finish)
- Silt fence / SWPPP / erosion control
- Tree protection fencing
- Construction signage
- Final clean (interior + exterior)
- Construction cleaning during build (rough, intermediate)
- Punch list reserve
- Project management / supervision
- Contingency (5–10% recommended)

## Hardware / Connectors
- Simpson hangers (joist, beam, post)
- Hold-downs (HDU, MSTC)
- Straps (LSTA, MSTA, CMSTC)
- Anchor bolts (qty + size)
- Fasteners by the lb (8d, 10d, 16d, ring shank)
- Sealants (silicone, polyurethane, butyl)
- Adhesives (PL, construction adhesive)
- Weatherstripping
- Caulking and backer rod

## Backing / Blocking
- Grab bars (bath walls, shower)
- Wall-mount TVs (bedrooms, living, primary)
- Cabinets (upper and base)
- Closet shelving
- Handrails (stairs, halls)
- Towel bars and accessories
- Curtain / drapery rods
- Attic ladder framing

## Code-Driven Adds
- AFCI breakers per code
- GFCI receptacles (kitchen, bath, exterior, garage, laundry, basement)
- Smoke/CO interconnect, hardwired with battery backup
- Fireblocking (concealed spaces, top/bottom of walls)
- Draftstopping (large concealed spaces)
- Egress windows (basements, bedrooms below grade)
- Tempered glass at hazardous locations (near doors, tubs, stairs)
- Ice & water shield by climate zone (eaves, valleys, penetrations)
- Fresh-air ventilation per IRC (whole-house ventilation)
- Whole-house ventilation control

## MEP Wedge Items
- Range hood duct to exterior (with damper)
- Bath fan duct to exterior with backdraft damper
- Dryer vent (with damper, exterior cap)
- Condensate float switch on HVAC
- Water hammer arrestors (washing machine, dishwasher)
- Frost-proof hose bibs (qty front/back/sides)
- Exterior GFCIs (front, back, sides)
- Dedicated appliance circuits (microwave, fridge, DW, disposal)
- Doorbell circuit + transformer
- Garage door opener outlets (per door)
- Attic light + outlet near furnace
- Photocell or timer for exterior lights
- Whole-house surge protector (some codes)

## Finish Wedge Items
- Shower waterproofing membrane (Schluter, Kerdi, RedGard)
- Tile substrate (cement board, uncoupling membrane)
- Anti-fracture membrane on cracked slabs
- Floor transitions and threshold strips
- Stair nosing
- Paint touch-up after flooring install
- Closet shelving and rods
- Door stops at every door
- Address numbers
- Mailbox + post + concrete
- Driveway approach / culvert (per local code)
- Final landscaping / sod / final grade
- Sidewalk and patio
