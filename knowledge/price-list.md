# Built-In Price List

Default unit prices for cost estimates. Three sections:
- **RESIDENTIAL** — single-family / multi-family wood frame work
- **COMMERCIAL** — structural steel, CFMF, commercial cladding, storefront, TPO/EPDM roofing, commercial MEP
- **CIVIL / SITEWORK** — paving, storm sewer, utilities, fencing, SWPPP

Use the section that matches the detected project type. Prices are starting points — tune to your local market and supplier quotes. All commercial and civil prices are Texas-typical 2024–2025 ranges; use the midpoint unless a quote tells you otherwise.
