# Trade Scope Reference

For each trade: **Includes** (standard scope), **Excludes** (commonly outside the bid), **Easily Missed** (items that turn into change orders if not addressed). Used by `/scope` and `/compare-sub` to find scope gaps in sub bids.

Three sections:
- **RESIDENTIAL TRADES** — wood frame work, residential MEP, residential finishes
- **COMMERCIAL TRADES** — structural steel, CFMF, commercial cladding, storefront, commercial roofing, commercial MEP, commercial finishes
- **CIVIL / SITEWORK TRADES** — earthwork, paving, storm sewer, fencing, SWPPP

---

# RESIDENTIAL TRADES

---

## Sitework / Excavation
- **Includes:** clearing, stripping topsoil, rough grade, excavation for foundation, backfill, final grade.
- **Excludes:** tree removal (large), rock removal, dewatering, off-site haul-off, retaining walls, septic, well, driveway base.
- **Easily missed:** silt fence / SWPPP, tree protection, soil testing, compaction testing, import/export of fill, erosion control, dust control.

## Foundation / Concrete
- **Includes:** layout, formwork, rebar, anchor bolts, concrete supply + place + finish, strip forms.
- **Excludes:** vapor barrier (verify), termite pretreat, sub-base prep, insulation under slab, waterproofing, dewatering, engineering.
- **Easily missed:** holdowns, sleeves for plumbing/electrical, control joints, sealer/cure, concrete pump fees, cold/hot weather concrete additives, slab penetration patches.

## Framing
- **Includes:** studs, plates, headers, beams, joists, rafters/trusses, sheathing, blocking, sub-fascia.
- **Excludes:** house wrap (sometimes), exterior trim, hardware/connectors (verify), LVLs/PSLs (sometimes by GC), stairs (sometimes specialty).
- **Easily missed:** Simpson hangers/hold-downs/straps, fasteners by the lb, fireblocking, draftstopping, backing for grab bars/TVs/cabinets, blocking for handrails, attic access framing, soffit framing.

## Roofing
- **Includes:** underlayment, shingles or metal, ridge cap, starter strip, drip edge, ridge vent, valley flashing, pipe boots, nails.
- **Excludes:** roof decking (often by framer), gutters/downspouts, skylights, solar, chimney flashing replacement.
- **Easily missed:** ice & water shield (eaves, valleys, penetrations), kickout flashing at roof-wall, step flashing, headwall flashing, walkable underlayment in valleys, hip/ridge shingle upcharge, dump fees on tear-off.

## Exterior (Siding / Windows / Exterior Doors)
- **Includes:** house wrap, siding, J-channel, starter, corner trim, fasteners, install of windows/doors set in openings.
- **Excludes:** rough openings (framer), interior trim of openings, hardware upgrade, painting, caulking color match, low-E/tempered upgrades.
- **Easily missed:** window flashing tape, sill pans, exterior caulk and backer rod, drip cap above openings, foam sealant around openings, threshold extensions, screens, hardware finish upgrade.

## Insulation
- **Includes:** walls, ceilings, sound batts where shown.
- **Excludes:** rim joist (verify), basement walls, crawlspace, attic baffles, vapor retarder where required.
- **Easily missed:** behind-tub insulation, exterior wall behind shower, garage ceiling above living, bonus room kneewalls, attic access cover insulation, can-light covers.

## Drywall
- **Includes:** hang, tape, mud, sand, level 4 finish, prime coat (sometimes).
- **Excludes:** paint finish coats, texture (verify), suspended ceilings, fire-rated assemblies upcharge, smooth ceilings (level 5).
- **Easily missed:** corner bead at every corner, control joints in long runs, water-resistant board in wet areas, fire-rated board at garage common wall, screws and adhesive, access panels.

## Painting
- **Includes:** prep, prime, two finish coats interior, two coats exterior, basic caulk/patch.
- **Excludes:** stain-grade work, cabinet finish, exterior touch-up after others, color-matching nail holes, pressure washing.
- **Easily missed:** closet interiors, garage interior, attic access, pulled trim re-paint after flooring, exterior soffit/fascia, exterior doors (both sides), railings.

## Plumbing
- **Includes:** water service connection, DWV rough, supply rough, gas line (sometimes), fixture set, water heater install.
- **Excludes:** well, septic field, fire sprinkler, water softener, recirculation pump, pressure-reducing valve, fixtures over allowance.
- **Easily missed:** hose bibs (qty + frost-proof), washing machine box, ice maker line, dishwasher tail, disposal wiring (electrical), tub/shower valve trim allowance, toilet supply lines, escutcheons, dielectric unions.

## Electrical
- **Includes:** service, panel, circuits, devices, basic fixtures, terminations, code-required AFCI/GFCI, smoke/CO interconnect.
- **Excludes:** low-voltage/data, security, EV charger, generator/transfer switch, fixtures over allowance, service upgrade if existing, solar.
- **Easily missed:** bath fan circuits, range hood circuit, dedicated appliance circuits (microwave, fridge, dishwasher, disposal), exterior GFCIs (front, back, sides), garage door opener outlets, attic light + outlet, doorbell, photocell for exterior lights.

## HVAC
- **Includes:** equipment supply + install, duct rough, refrigerant line set, registers/grilles, thermostat, condensate drain.
- **Excludes:** gas piping (verify, often plumber), electrical disconnect (electrician), condensate pump, zoning controls (sometimes), humidifier, ERV/HRV, ductwork insulation upcharge.
- **Easily missed:** bath fan ducting to exterior with backdraft damper, range hood duct to exterior, dryer vent, fresh-air intake (code in many jurisdictions), float switch on condensate, manual J load calc, equipment pad, attic catwalk to equipment.

## Cabinets & Countertops
- **Includes:** boxes, doors, drawers, install, basic toe kick, hardware (verify allowance).
- **Excludes:** countertop fabrication, sink cutout, faucet install (plumber), backsplash, under-cabinet lighting (electrician), trash pull-out hardware upgrade.
- **Easily missed:** filler strips, scribe molding, crown above wall cabinets, light rail under wall cabinets, soft-close upgrade, drawer box upgrade, panel-ready appliance panels, end panels exposed to view.

## Tile
- **Includes:** thin-set, tile, grout, basic edge profile.
- **Excludes:** substrate (cement board / Schluter), waterproofing membrane, shower pan, niche framing, decorative accent tile premium, sealing.
- **Easily missed:** Schluter/Kerdi waterproofing, RedGard, pre-formed shower pans, niche, curb, bench, transitions to other flooring, grout sealer, anti-fracture membrane, expansion joints in long runs.

## Flooring (LVP / Hardwood / Carpet)
- **Includes:** install, basic underlayment, transitions where specified.
- **Excludes:** subfloor leveling/prep, baseboard removal/replace, moisture testing, acclimation period, stain or finish on raw hardwood (sometimes).
- **Easily missed:** floor leveling compound, T-moldings/reducers/end caps, stair nosing, underlayment upgrade, vapor barrier on slab, tack strip, transitions at door openings, threshold transitions.

## Interior Trim & Doors
- **Includes:** door slabs, jambs, casing, base, hardware install.
- **Excludes:** stain-grade upgrade, special hardware, closet shelving, door hardware finish upgrade, wainscoting/paneling.
- **Easily missed:** door stops, silencers, threshold at exterior doors, weatherstripping, closet rods/shelves, mirror doors, pocket door hardware, barn door tracks, return air grilles in jambs.

# COMMERCIAL TRADES

---

## Structural Steel
- **Includes:** material (HSS, W-sections, channels, angles, plates), shop fabrication, shop primer, delivery, erection, bolted/welded connections, base plates.
- **Excludes:** anchor bolts (often by GC/concrete sub for embed), special inspections, weld testing (UT/MT/PT), galvanizing (often add), AESS upcharge, fireproofing, intumescent paint, miscellaneous metals (handrails, ladders, embeds), light gauge framing.
- **Easily missed:** AESS Category 1-4 upcharges (huge — 25-100% more), shop drawings/submittals time, expediting fees, third-party special inspections (paid by GC), high-strength bolts spec, slip-critical bolting, weld testing (UT for welds over thickness), erection sequence engineering, bracing during erection, paint touch-up at field welds, embeds and edge angles to other trades, kicker connections to existing deck/framing.

## Cold-Formed Metal Framing (CFMF)
- **Includes:** structural studs and tracks per spec gauge, screws, bridging, clip angles, connection to structure.
- **Excludes:** load-bearing CFMF engineering, sheathing (often by drywall/cladding sub), insulation, vapor barrier, exterior finish.
- **Easily missed:** stud gauge upgrade where shown on plans (e.g., 54mil structural at openings), CRC channel (cold-rolled), kicker bracing to deck, embeds, deflection track at top of wall, head-of-wall fire/smoke joint, jamb framing at storefront/openings (heavy gauge), backing for cabinets/grab bars, clip angles to steel structure.

## Commercial Cladding (Nichiha / ACM / EIFS / Stucco / Adhered Stone / Metal Panel)
- **Includes:** primary cladding panels, panel clips/fasteners, trim profiles, starter strips, basic weather barrier (verify), perimeter sealant.
- **Excludes:** CFMF or sheathing substrate, weather-resistive barrier (sometimes by framer), flashing at openings (verify), backer rod, secondary water management, painting, ACM panel engineering (sometimes by manufacturer).
- **Easily missed:** transition flashings at material changes (e.g., Nichiha to stucco), drip edge above openings, J-channel and starter, panel layout/coursing approval, seam joint sealant (separate from perimeter), corner profiles, returns at openings, soffit panels, water table cap stone, mock-up panel approval, color/finish sample approval.

## Aluminum Storefront / Curtainwall
- **Includes:** aluminum framing system, glazing (IGUs), perimeter sealant, doors with closers, basic ADA hardware, anchorage to substrate.
- **Excludes:** rough opening preparation, perimeter blocking (CFMF or wood), interior trim, exterior caulking color match, automatic operators, security (mag locks, card readers), security film, electrical for automatic doors, threshold to flooring transition.
- **Easily missed:** tempered glass at hazardous locations, spandrel panels at concealed areas, sunshade/shading devices, perimeter air seal, weep holes, thermal break verification, blocking and back-anchorage, ADA threshold height (1/2" max), panic hardware, electric strike for access control rough-in.

## Commercial Roofing (TPO / EPDM / Mod-Bit)
- **Includes:** membrane, tapered insulation, cover board, fasteners or adhesive, perimeter edge metal, roof drains/scuppers, basic flashings at penetrations, walkway pads at servicing equipment.
- **Excludes:** roof deck (steel deck by structural), wood blocking (often by GC/carpenter), parapet wall framing, RTUs and curbs (HVAC), structural concrete pads, lightning protection, solar.
- **Easily missed:** RTU curbs and curb extensions, pipe penetration flashings (qty), plumbing vent flashings, scupper/conductor heads, expansion joints across structural movement, walkway pads at servicing routes, snow guards (climate), bird/critter exclusion at vents, NDL warranty (no-dollar-limit) inspection visits, infrared scan, manufacturer-required pre-roofing meeting.

## Commercial MEP — Electrical
- **Includes:** service entrance, switchgear/panels, distribution, branch circuits, devices, light fixtures (per allowance), terminations, fire alarm rough (sometimes), code-compliant grounding.
- **Excludes:** low-voltage / data / AV (separate vendor), security/access control, fire alarm (often separate), generator, ATS, EV charger infrastructure, exterior lighting beyond building (sometimes site lighting separate), photovoltaic, kitchen equipment final connections.
- **Easily missed:** transformer pad and conduits (utility coordination), CT cabinet, surge protective device, panel directory, arc-flash labels, GFCI/AFCI per code, exterior receptacles weatherproof, night lighting circuits, exit/emergency lighting tied to circuits, generator interlock, photocell or astronomic timer for site lighting, RTU disconnects at each unit (qty).

## Commercial MEP — Plumbing
- **Includes:** water service, sanitary lateral, gas lateral, DWV rough, supply rough, fixtures (commercial flush valve toilets/urinals/sinks), water heater, hose bibs, floor drains.
- **Excludes:** site utilities outside building (5 ft from building line — civil), grease interceptor (sometimes), fire sprinkler, water softener, RO system, kitchen equipment final connections, gas line for HVAC equipment (sometimes mechanical sub).
- **Easily missed:** trap primers at floor drains, mop sink and water hammer arrestors, hose bibs (qty + frost-proof), backflow preventer at water service (RPZ assembly), grease interceptor sizing, indirect waste from kitchen equipment, condensate drains from HVAC tied to plumbing, roof drain leaders inside building, recirculation pump for hot water (commercial), expansion tank.

## Commercial MEP — HVAC
- **Includes:** equipment (RTUs, splits, VRF), ductwork, refrigerant lines, registers/grilles, controls/thermostats, condensate, basic filtration, code-required ventilation.
- **Excludes:** electrical disconnects (electrician), gas piping to equipment (verify, often plumber), structural curbs (roofer/structural), TAB (test & balance — sometimes separate), commissioning, BMS (building management system) integration, equipment screening.
- **Easily missed:** outside air calculation per ASHRAE 62.1 for occupancy, economizer where required by climate, smoke detectors in ductwork (>2,000 cfm), fire dampers at fire-rated walls/floors, smoke dampers at smoke barriers, condensate float switches and pumps, RTU service walkway, roof curb height per local code (typically 8-14"), CO sensors at parking/loading, kitchen exhaust hood (if applicable — separate scope).

## Commercial Finishes
- **Includes:** ACT ceilings, VCT/sealed concrete/polished concrete/epoxy floors per spec, wall base, hollow metal doors and frames, commercial door hardware.
- **Excludes:** demising wall framing (CFMF), drywall (separate), painting (separate), specialty millwork, casework, signage, lockers.
- **Easily missed:** fire-rated ceiling systems where required, ACT cuts/borders at perimeter, transitions between flooring types, expansion joints in slab transferred to flooring, hollow metal frame anchoring (welded vs. masonry vs. CFMF), 3-hinge vs 5-knuckle door hardware, ADA-compliant lever hardware, door silencers, kickplates, weatherstripping at exterior doors, threshold finish.

## Fireproofing / Firestop
- **Includes:** spray-applied fireproofing on structural members (1-hr or 2-hr per plans), firestop at penetrations through rated walls/floors, fire/smoke joint sealants.
- **Excludes:** intumescent paint (separate scope, often architectural), structural steel surface prep, fireproofing at AESS (intumescent), penetration coordination with MEP trades.
- **Easily missed:** field touch-up after MEP penetrations, top-of-wall (head-of-wall) joint at smoke/fire barriers, expansion joints in fire-rated assemblies, perimeter fire containment at curtainwall (Cat I, II), thickness verification at critical members, third-party inspection of fireproofing thickness.

# CIVIL / SITEWORK TRADES

---

## Earthwork / Mass Grading
- **Includes:** strip topsoil, mass excavation, fill placement and compaction, finish grading to plan elevations.
- **Excludes:** import/export of fill (verify cy and direction), rock excavation (allowance), unsuitable soil removal, dewatering, retaining walls, lime/cement stabilization.
- **Easily missed:** import or export quantities (always verify cut/fill balance), unsuitable soil overdig and re-fill, dewatering pump rental, storm event delays during earthwork, final grade tolerance per civil plans, topsoil stockpile and respread, compaction testing (paid by GC typically).

## Concrete Paving (Commercial / Civil)
- **Includes:** concrete supply + place + finish, rebar per plans, control joints, expansion joints, curing compound, basic substrate prep over compacted base.
- **Excludes:** lime-stabilized subgrade (separate), aggregate base course (separate), dowels at construction joints (verify), striping and signage, ADA truncated dome plates (sometimes separate vendor).
- **Easily missed:** dowel basket assemblies at long pours, isolation joints at structures (curbs, manholes, light pole bases), backer rod and joint sealant at expansion joints (often forgotten), curing time before opening to traffic, weather-related schedule risk (heat/cold limits), thickened slab edges at heavy load areas, surface texture finish (broom direction), saw cut timing for control joints (4-12 hrs).

## Storm Sewer / Utilities
- **Includes:** pipe (per spec material and class), bedding stone, backfill, manholes/inlets/headwalls, connections to existing systems.
- **Excludes:** trench shoring (depth-dependent), dewatering during install, pavement removal/replacement above trench, surveying for line/grade, video inspection (post-install), connection to franchise utility infrastructure (TxDOT, county).
- **Easily missed:** trench shoring at depths >5 ft (OSHA), bedding stone qty (often #57 or #67 stone), connection to existing manhole (core drill + boot), drop manhole at large elevation changes, manhole frame and cover finished elevation matching pavement, inlet grates traffic rating (HS-20 for vehicular), riprap at outfall with filter fabric, dye testing or video inspection after install.

## SWPPP / Erosion Control
- **Includes:** silt fence install + maintenance, stabilized construction entrance, inlet protection, concrete washout, periodic SWPPP inspections during construction.
- **Excludes:** SWPPP plan preparation (engineer), Notice of Intent (NOI) filing, final stabilization (hydromulch/sod — often separate), permanent BMPs (detention pond, rain garden — separate scope).
- **Easily missed:** SWPPP plan and SWPPP inspection logs (QSP/QSD if required), repairs to silt fence after rain events, additional inlet protection as inlets are added, dust control during dry weather, off-site mud tracking cleanup, final removal of SWPPP measures and disposal.

## Detention Pond / Stormwater Management
- **Includes:** pond mass excavation, shaping, outlet structure (concrete riser/headwall), gravity outfall pipe, basic pond bottom prep.
- **Excludes:** liner (clay or synthetic, if required), permanent stabilization (hydromulch/sod on slopes), pump station (separate scope), outfall energy dissipation (riprap), water quality features (forebay, micropool).
- **Easily missed:** anti-seep collars on outfall pipe through embankment, embankment compaction testing, freeboard verification, emergency overflow weir, pond bottom slope toward outlet, side-slope stabilization (typically 4:1 max for mowing access), permanent maintenance access road into pond.

## Pump Station (Stormwater / Lift)
- **Includes:** wet well, pumps (per spec — Flygt NP3127 or equivalent), guide rails, level controls/floats, control panel, force main connection.
- **Excludes:** electrical service to pump station (electrician), generator (sometimes separate), telemetry/SCADA (sometimes separate), wet well structural design (engineer), startup and commissioning (sometimes separate).
- **Easily missed:** wet well ventilation, hatch frame and lid (H-20 traffic rating if in pavement), odor control if specified, redundant pump per code (duplex required for most jurisdictions), high-water alarm float, anti-flotation calculations for empty wet well, force main air release valve at high points.

## Site Fencing / Gates
- **Includes:** fence posts, fabric/panels, top rail, ties, gates per qty, concrete footings.
- **Excludes:** electrical for automatic gate operators, access control (card readers, keypads, intercoms), site lighting at gates, painting (galvanized typically standard), vinyl-coating upgrade.
- **Easily missed:** corner and end posts heavier than line posts, gate posts with concrete footings sized larger, gate operator electrical conduit, safety devices on automatic gates (photo eyes, edges), exit detection loop at automated gates, fall protection during install at heights, alignment and post depth in heavy clay or rocky soil.

## Striping & Pavement Markings
- **Includes:** thermoplastic or paint per spec, stalls, fire lanes, ADA symbols, stop bars, directional arrows.
- **Excludes:** signage (separate vendor), pavement removal/grinding for re-stripe, traffic control during application.
- **Easily missed:** ADA-required parking count, ADA van-accessible space sizing (8-ft access aisle, 11-ft stall), striping cure time before traffic, fire lane painting per fire marshal spec, no-parking zones and red curbs.
