# Trade Scope Reference

For each trade: **Includes** (standard scope), **Excludes** (commonly outside the bid), **Easily Missed** (items that turn into change orders if not addressed). Used by `/scope` and `/compare-sub` to find scope gaps in sub bids.

---

## Sitework / Excavation
- **Includes:** clearing, stripping topsoil, rough grade, excavation for foundation, backfill, final grade.
- **Excludes:** tree removal (large), rock removal, dewatering, off-site haul-off, retaining walls, septic, well, driveway base.
- **Easily missed:** silt fence / SWPPP, tree protection, soil testing, compaction testing, import/export of fill, erosion control, dust control.

## Foundation / Concrete
- **Includes:** layout, formwork, rebar, anchor bolts, concrete supply + place + finish, strip forms.
- **Excludes:** vapor barrier (verify), termite pretreat, sub-base prep, insulation under slab, waterproofing, dewatering, engineering.
- **Easily missed:** holdowns, sleeves for plumbing/electrical, control joints, sealer/cure, concrete pump fees, cold/hot weather concrete additives, slab penetration patches.

## Framing
- **Includes:** studs, plates, headers, beams, joists, rafters/trusses, sheathing, blocking, sub-fascia.
- **Excludes:** house wrap (sometimes), exterior trim, hardware/connectors (verify), LVLs/PSLs (sometimes by GC), stairs (sometimes specialty).
- **Easily missed:** Simpson hangers/hold-downs/straps, fasteners by the lb, fireblocking, draftstopping, backing for grab bars/TVs/cabinets, blocking for handrails, attic access framing, soffit framing.

## Roofing
- **Includes:** underlayment, shingles or metal, ridge cap, starter strip, drip edge, ridge vent, valley flashing, pipe boots, nails.
- **Excludes:** roof decking (often by framer), gutters/downspouts, skylights, solar, chimney flashing replacement.
- **Easily missed:** ice & water shield (eaves, valleys, penetrations), kickout flashing at roof-wall, step flashing, headwall flashing, walkable underlayment in valleys, hip/ridge shingle upcharge, dump fees on tear-off.

## Exterior (Siding / Windows / Exterior Doors)
- **Includes:** house wrap, siding, J-channel, starter, corner trim, fasteners, install of windows/doors set in openings.
- **Excludes:** rough openings (framer), interior trim of openings, hardware upgrade, painting, caulking color match, low-E/tempered upgrades.
- **Easily missed:** window flashing tape, sill pans, exterior caulk and backer rod, drip cap above openings, foam sealant around openings, threshold extensions, screens, hardware finish upgrade.

## Insulation
- **Includes:** walls, ceilings, sound batts where shown.
- **Excludes:** rim joist (verify), basement walls, crawlspace, attic baffles, vapor retarder where required.
- **Easily missed:** behind-tub insulation, exterior wall behind shower, garage ceiling above living, bonus room kneewalls, attic access cover insulation, can-light covers.

## Drywall
- **Includes:** hang, tape, mud, sand, level 4 finish, prime coat (sometimes).
- **Excludes:** paint finish coats, texture (verify), suspended ceilings, fire-rated assemblies upcharge, smooth ceilings (level 5).
- **Easily missed:** corner bead at every corner, control joints in long runs, water-resistant board in wet areas, fire-rated board at garage common wall, screws and adhesive, access panels.

## Painting
- **Includes:** prep, prime, two finish coats interior, two coats exterior, basic caulk/patch.
- **Excludes:** stain-grade work, cabinet finish, exterior touch-up after others, color-matching nail holes, pressure washing.
- **Easily missed:** closet interiors, garage interior, attic access, pulled trim re-paint after flooring, exterior soffit/fascia, exterior doors (both sides), railings.

## Plumbing
- **Includes:** water service connection, DWV rough, supply rough, gas line (sometimes), fixture set, water heater install.
- **Excludes:** well, septic field, fire sprinkler, water softener, recirculation pump, pressure-reducing valve, fixtures over allowance.
- **Easily missed:** hose bibs (qty + frost-proof), washing machine box, ice maker line, dishwasher tail, disposal wiring (electrical), tub/shower valve trim allowance, toilet supply lines, escutcheons, dielectric unions.

## Electrical
- **Includes:** service, panel, circuits, devices, basic fixtures, terminations, code-required AFCI/GFCI, smoke/CO interconnect.
- **Excludes:** low-voltage/data, security, EV charger, generator/transfer switch, fixtures over allowance, service upgrade if existing, solar.
- **Easily missed:** bath fan circuits, range hood circuit, dedicated appliance circuits (microwave, fridge, dishwasher, disposal), exterior GFCIs (front, back, sides), garage door opener outlets, attic light + outlet, doorbell, photocell for exterior lights.

## HVAC
- **Includes:** equipment supply + install, duct rough, refrigerant line set, registers/grilles, thermostat, condensate drain.
- **Excludes:** gas piping (verify, often plumber), electrical disconnect (electrician), condensate pump, zoning controls (sometimes), humidifier, ERV/HRV, ductwork insulation upcharge.
- **Easily missed:** bath fan ducting to exterior with backdraft damper, range hood duct to exterior, dryer vent, fresh-air intake (code in many jurisdictions), float switch on condensate, manual J load calc, equipment pad, attic catwalk to equipment.

## Cabinets & Countertops
- **Includes:** boxes, doors, drawers, install, basic toe kick, hardware (verify allowance).
- **Excludes:** countertop fabrication, sink cutout, faucet install (plumber), backsplash, under-cabinet lighting (electrician), trash pull-out hardware upgrade.
- **Easily missed:** filler strips, scribe molding, crown above wall cabinets, light rail under wall cabinets, soft-close upgrade, drawer box upgrade, panel-ready appliance panels, end panels exposed to view.

## Tile
- **Includes:** thin-set, tile, grout, basic edge profile.
- **Excludes:** substrate (cement board / Schluter), waterproofing membrane, shower pan, niche framing, decorative accent tile premium, sealing.
- **Easily missed:** Schluter/Kerdi waterproofing, RedGard, pre-formed shower pans, niche, curb, bench, transitions to other flooring, grout sealer, anti-fracture membrane, expansion joints in long runs.

## Flooring (LVP / Hardwood / Carpet)
- **Includes:** install, basic underlayment, transitions where specified.
- **Excludes:** subfloor leveling/prep, baseboard removal/replace, moisture testing, acclimation period, stain or finish on raw hardwood (sometimes).
- **Easily missed:** floor leveling compound, T-moldings/reducers/end caps, stair nosing, underlayment upgrade, vapor barrier on slab, tack strip, transitions at door openings, threshold transitions.

## Interior Trim & Doors
- **Includes:** door slabs, jambs, casing, base, hardware install.
- **Excludes:** stain-grade upgrade, special hardware, closet shelving, door hardware finish upgrade, wainscoting/paneling.
- **Easily missed:** door stops, silencers, threshold at exterior doors, weatherstripping, closet rods/shelves, mirror doors, pocket door hardware, barn door tracks, return air grilles in jambs.
